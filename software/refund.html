Refund Policy

You can request a refund for any reason within 14 days of purchase. To request one, go to paddle.net or use the link on your receipt. Our payment provider, Paddle, processes refunds on our behalf, and approved refunds go back to your original payment method.

We recommend trying the free trial first to check that the software works with your archives. The trial reads your archives the same way the licensed version does and exports up to 24 hours of data per run.

Problems with the software

If the software does not work with your archives, email bob.meads@iqagent.com with your order number and a description of the problem. We will work with you to resolve it. You can still request a refund within the 14 days.

Renewals

The same 14-day window applies to renewal payments for updates. A refund ends your license and your access to updates.

Your rights under Paddle’s terms and under applicable law are not affected by this policy.
